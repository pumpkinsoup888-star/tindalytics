Before you message me

Things business owners always ask.

Honest answers, no fine print.

Magkano lahat? May hidden fees ba? +
The package price you see is the total cost of building your website. There are no hidden fees and no monthly fees. I keep your site online for free, for as long as you want it. The only ongoing cost is your domain renewal, around ₱900 a year, paid directly to your domain provider, never to me. If you ever want to host the site yourself, I will hand over all the files so you can.
Paano kung hindi ko gusto ang output? +
Two rounds of revisions are included with every package. After the first draft, you can request changes to the layout, colors, copy, or anything else. We refine it until you are happy. The remaining 50% balance is only paid after you approve the final design, so you are never locked into something you do not love.
Kailangan ko bang marunong sa tech? +
Not at all. The whole point of this service is that you do not need to learn anything technical. I handle the domain, hosting, design, build, and Google setup. You give me your business info and photos, and you receive a finished website. If you ever want to update something small, I will show you exactly how, or do it for you during your support period.
What if I want to edit the website myself someday? +
You can. Your website is yours and it is built on simple HTML, so you have full access to make changes. If you ever want to host it yourself, I will hand over all the files and help you move it. Most clients prefer to just message me for small edits like menu prices, opening hours, or contact details. Small changes are included during your 14 day support period after launch. After that, I offer an optional maintenance service for ongoing edits, or I can show you how to do small changes yourself.
Paano ako babayad? +
Payment is via GCash. A 50% deposit confirms your slot and starts the project. The remaining 50% is paid only after you approve the final design, right before we launch. This protects both of us. You only finish paying once you are happy with the result.
How many websites do you take at a time? +
I only take on two to three website projects at a time so that each client gets focused attention and the 7 day turnaround can actually be met. If a slot is not available right away, I will tell you honestly and let you know when the next one opens.
